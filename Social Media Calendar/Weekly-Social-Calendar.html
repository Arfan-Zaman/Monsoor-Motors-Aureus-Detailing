Before anything goes live

People and plates

  • Blur every customer number plate.
  • Written OK from customers before showing their car or face.
  • Staff OK before they appear in a video.

Truth (Australian Consumer Law)

  • No fake reviews, testimonials or results.
  • Prices match the listing and booking page: drive-away, no hidden extras.
  • No invented numbers or offers. Only post an offer the owner has agreed.

AI content

  • Turn on the "AI-generated" label for AI video or images on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook.
  • Monsoor feed posts use real photos only, so they carry no AI label.
  • Never change a real car's colour, condition or features.

Format

  • Reels 9:16, 1080×1920, text clear of the top 250 px and bottom 400 px.
  • Feed photos 1080×1350. Montserrat, brand colours only.
  • Hook in the first 2 seconds. Music added in the app at low volume.
