# "See you less, bowser": 29 s reel, production plan (v2)

Status: story plan for Arfan's approval, 21 Nov 2026. Nothing rendered or generated for this version. Credit cap: 250.
Placement: Meta (Facebook and Instagram Reels), 9:16, 1080x1920, captions burned in. Goal: bring Perth people into the Monsoor Motors world before the campaign. No campaign mention.

## 1. Creative
**Insight.** Perth families don't dread buying a car; they dread the weekly fill-up, and they don't trust car ads (WA Consumer Protection keeps warning about fake sellers on Facebook).
**Idea.** A small, true, funny story: a Perth dad's weekly break-up with the bowser, made possible by two blokes in Victoria Park who hand-pick hybrids from Japan's auctions, check every one, and bring it to his door.
**Tone.** Real people, real places, dry Aussie humour. Show, don't tell: nobody announces job titles. The founder and the new CEO are shown working together; the generational contrast (old-school founder, AI-savvy new CEO) is the gentle comedy.
**How we beat the market.** Perth import and hybrid dealers advertise with stock listings and claims ("licensed importer", "certified inspection", "1-year warranty", "largest yard"). Nobody tells a story, shows real faces, or brings the car home. We do all three, and we back the claims with what we really do: every car inspected, warranty included, auction report kept, delivered.

## 5. Production method (Higgsfield tutorial method, adapted)
1. Approve this story. 2. Re-block the Blender previs to this story (free). 3. Make the new sheets at medium quality (1 credit each), three-quarter angles, grey-background character sheets, tack-sharp with real texture. 4. Test pair stills at medium (1 credit each). 5. Scene generations with Seedance 2.5 (omni reference: start frame + elements + locked voice clip as audio reference): 480p draft first, then 720p final of the winner. 6. Edit in-house: cut on movement, captions, supers, end card, sound design and licensed music, loudness -14 LUFS. Upscale to 1080p on this Mac (free).
