### Like-for-like problems (be upfront)

1. **Different testers, different routes.** RACV did 3 weeks of country and city driving. CarsGuide's Tucson figure came from a long-term loan. CarExpert's are week loans, and some are "indicated" (trip computer), not measured at the pump. These are not one controlled test.
2. **Different model years and markets.** RACV tested a 2020 Australian-delivered C-HR Hybrid. Ours is a **2018 import** with the same 1.8 hybrid system, but the import's spec and condition may differ. Don't claim RACV tested our car.
3. **Size class.** The C-HR is a small SUV. The Tucson, X-Trail, Sportage and Outlander are mid-size, and some have 7 seats. A sharp viewer will call this "not the same car". The honest angle is "same money", not "same car".
4. **Driving mix matters.** Hybrids do best in stop-start city traffic and narrow the gap on the highway. "Half" is a mixed-driving figure, not a guarantee.
5. **Price basis.** The rival prices exclude government charges. Our C-HR price is drive-away.
