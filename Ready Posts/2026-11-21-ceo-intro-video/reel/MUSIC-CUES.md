# "Queue Jump": music and sound, shot by shot (39 s short film / ad)

**Score:** one original, instrumental 80s-tinged driving track at **120 BPM** (1 bar = 2.0 s, 1 beat = 0.5 s), in **D minor → lifting to E major for the morning**. Gated drums, analogue synth bass, warm chorus guitar, a bright synth lead. Premium, cheeky, cinematic. No vocals, no imitation of any real song.
**Signature motif:** the C-HR's soft "READY" chime (three notes, D–F#–A) is also the synth hook, so the car's sound and the music are one idea. It returns on the logo.
**How we make it without blunders:** generate the score as **3 separate sections** (not one long track), then cut and sync them to the picture frame-accurately in our own edit. One long AI track never lands on cuts; sections do.
