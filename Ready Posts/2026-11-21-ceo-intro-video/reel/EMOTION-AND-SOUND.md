## 3. Music direction
- Two cues from one ElevenLabs Music v2.5 instrumental generation (2 versions, 8.44 credits): M1 = the tense, playful servo pulse; M2 = the confident warm groove. Same key and tempo (about 100 bpm) so the cut at 8.0 s and the return at 11.0 s land on the beat.
- Cut picture on the beat where the action allows (door clunk at 11.0, keys at 19.3, home at 23.5).

## 4. Mix
Dialogue first and clean; music about 13 dB under dialogue, louder only in the gaps; ambience under every shot except the deliberate silence gag; final loudness −14 LUFS integrated, peaks below −1 dBTP (Meta / Instagram). Check on phone speakers and on headphones.
