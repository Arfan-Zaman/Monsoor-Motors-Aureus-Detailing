# Arfan: voice sample lines, take 2 (for the voice clone `Arfan-CEO-2`)

Read these the way you would speak to a customer on camera: confident, warm, medium pace. Quiet room, microphone about 20 cm away. Pause for a breath between lines. About 45 seconds.

1. Good morning, and welcome. My name is Arfan, and I look after things here in Victoria Park.
2. People ask me the same question every week: how do I know this car is a good one?
3. It's a fair question. You work hard for your money, and you deserve a straight answer.
4. So we take our time. We look underneath, we read the history, and we drive it ourselves.
5. If something isn't right, we fix it, or we don't sell it. It really is that simple.
6. Say this one with full energy: That's the one! That is exactly the car you've been looking for!
7. And finish calm and friendly: Have a look around, ask me anything, and take all the time you need.

None of these lines is used in the video. They are written in the same tone as your script so the clone learns how you sound on camera.
