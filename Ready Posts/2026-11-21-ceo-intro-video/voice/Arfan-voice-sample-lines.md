# Arfan: voice sample lines (for the voice clone)

Read these out loud in your normal speaking voice, in a quiet room, phone or laptop about 20 cm from your mouth. Do not whisper and do not act. Take a short breath between lines. About 40 seconds in total.

1. Hello, this is Arfan, and I'm recording this from Victoria Park in Perth.
2. I had a strong coffee this morning, checked my messages, and drove to the yard before eight.
3. Some days are quiet. Some days the phone doesn't stop, and honestly, I like those days better.
4. Is it going to rain this afternoon? I really hope not, because we've got cars to wash.
5. Seven, eighteen, twenty-three, forty-six, ninety-two, and two thousand and twenty-six.
6. Now a bit louder, with energy: This is the one! Come and have a look, you're going to love it!
7. And calm again: Thank you for listening. Take your time, there's no rush at all.

Why these lines: they mix a statement, a question, numbers, a loud excited line and a calm line, so the clone learns your full tone. None of them is used in the video.
