# Japanese Hybrid Imports: Ad Scripts (built from real data)

**For:** Monsoor Motors · **Written:** 24 Nov 2026 · **Status:** Ready to film after owner approval
**Goes with:** `Hybrid-Import-Meta-Ad-Plan.md` (budget, targeting, compliance)

Anything in **[square brackets]** is a real number we fill in on the day: price, km, rego, fuel figure. **Never guess these.** An invented number in a car ad is misleading conduct under the Australian Consumer Law.

---

## Part 1: What the data says works (and how each ad uses it)

### 1. Hook tactics that win on Meta
Motion's 2026 creative benchmark ranks 25+ hook types by how often they produce winning ads. The top ones produce winners **6–11%** of the time, against 4–8% on average.

| Hook type | Why it matters for us | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| **Announcement / Newness** (top of the list) | "Something just changed" stops the scroll | Ad 1 (import rule change), Ad 5 (just landed) |
| **Price anchor** (#3 for winners, #2 for spend) | Show the new-car price first, then ours | Ad 2 |
| **Urgency / FOMO** (top 5) | Real urgency: import approvals are actually ending | Ad 1 |
| **Confession** (steady all year round, best with a founder on camera) | "I walk away from most cars" builds trust | Ad 3 |
| **Myth busting / Warning** (pattern interrupt) | "Do hybrid batteries die?" is the #1 buyer fear | Ad 4 |
| **Contrast** | Old petrol car at the bowser vs a hybrid | Ad 6 |
| **Offer only** | Plain "we buy hybrids" | Ad 7 |

Motion's data window included Black Friday, so the promotional hooks are slightly inflated. **Confession, Warning and Myth-busting hooks stay strong all year.** So we run a mix, never all "sale" hooks.

### 2. Format: for dealers, Reels and carousels win, single photos lose
Emplifi Q3 2026 auto dealership benchmarks:
- **Instagram:** Reels led, with carousels close behind. Both earned **well over 2×** the interactions of a single image. Reels grew to 43.8% of dealer posts.
- **Facebook:** **Live video earned 3×+** the interactions of any other format, yet only 0.2% of dealers use it. That's an open lane (see Ad 5B).
- The top dealer posts of the quarter: 5 of 6 were Reels, and **humour** and personality did best.
→ **Every ad below is a Reel or a carousel.** The only single static is the "We buy" ad.

### 3. First 3 seconds and sound-off
- Meta's own research: brand and message in the **first 5 seconds** makes an ad **1.7×** more likely to land in the top purchase-intent group, and showing the brand across several scenes gave **1.8×**. → Logo watermark top-left the whole time, and the car and "Monsoor Motors" visible early.
- Meta tracks **hook rate** (3-second views ÷ impressions). Target: **30%+**.
- Most people watch muted. → **Burned-in captions on every video**, big, in the centre safe zone.

### 4. Why honest content goes viral in car sales
- The car videos that actually spread (500k+ views) are **price-transparency** clips (the "out-the-door price" dad) and **insiders explaining how the trade works**. A 2024 KPA survey: **76% of Americans don't trust dealers to be honest about pricing.** The same distrust exists here.
- → Our edge: **Monsoor is the honest insider.** Show the auction sheet, show the real price, say why we walk away.

### 5. What competitors are running right now (Meta Ad Library, checked 24 Nov 2026)
- **Japanese import ads in Australia** are mostly long spec lists, "!!!!!Dealer Sales!!!!!", "Serious buyers only", and no face, story or hook. (Brisbane and Adelaide sellers; 2024 Crown Sport, Voxy, 2008–2010 Crown Hybrid.)
- **UniBee (Melbourne)** is the most professional: offer-led ("FREE 1-Year Roadside Assistance, offer ends 30 Nov", "5-year warranty", "35+ years importing from Osaka").
- **Perth:** no Japanese-hybrid dealer was running video ads for "hybrid Perth". The closest was *Uncle Billys Detailing* running an EV/hybrid ceramic-coating lead ad (relevant to Aureus).
→ **No one in Perth owns "the Japanese hybrid people" on Facebook yet.** A real face, a real auction sheet and real news will stand out next to spec-list ads.

## Part 2: The scripts

**Every video:** 9:16, 1080×1920, shot on a phone (handheld, with natural light and room sound, per the CLAUDE.md editing rules). Logo watermark top-left at ~55%. Captions burned in (Montserrat Bold, warm white with a navy shadow). Nothing in the top 250 px or bottom 400 px. End card: logo · 0434 137 393 · monsoormotors.com.au · **MV Dealership Licence MD32748**. Plates blurred. Real cars only, no AI cars.

**On camera:** Monsoor (owner, founder format) for Ads 1, 3 and 4. For voiceover, use Arfan's locked voice track. Keep it natural: one take, small stumbles are fine. Being real is the point.

### AD 1: "The door is closing" · Reel 30–35 s · Hook: Announcement + Warning + Urgency
**Job:** the main lead ad. Real news nobody in Perth is telling buyers.

**Hook options (test all three, film the first 3 s three ways):**
- A: *"The government just changed the rules on Japanese hybrids. Here's what it means for you."*
- B: *"If you want a cheap Japanese hybrid, you're running out of time. This isn't a sales line."*
- C: (text on screen first, Monsoor silent, tapping the car roof) **"Japan's hybrids are getting harder to bring in."**

| Time | Picture | Monsoor says / on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Close: Monsoor beside a C-HR Hybrid on the yard, looks straight at the lens | Hook A/B/C. Text: **JAPANESE HYBRID IMPORTS ARE CLOSING** |
| 3–7 s | He walks along the car; quick cut to a hand holding an auction sheet | "Since December, Australia's new emissions rule means a lot of Japanese hybrids can't get new import approval." |
| 7–10 s | Phone screen: Drive headline (screen-recorded, readable) | Text: *Source: Drive, 29 Apr 2026* |
| 10–15 s | Close detail: hybrid badge, then dash readout | "The Prius approval has already run out. Others have an end date and can't be renewed." |
| 15–19 s | Wide: the yard, a few cars | "Cars already here are fine. They register like normal." |
| 19–27 s | Back to Monsoor, closer, a small push-in | "Some models we can still bring in, for now. Tell me what you're after and I'll tell you straight if it's still possible." |
| 27–32 s | End card | Text: **Ask which hybrids we can still import** · button *Get quote* |

**Primary text (caption):**
> Japanese hybrid imports are closing. 🚗
> Since December 2025, a new Australian emissions rule stops new import approvals for many Japanese hybrids. Some (like the current Prius) have already run out. Others have an end date.
> Cars already in Australia aren't affected.
> We can still bring in some models, for now. Tell us the car you want and we'll tell you honestly whether it's still possible.
> 📍 530 Albany Hwy, Victoria Park · 📞 0434 137 393
> Monsoor Motors · MV Dealership Licence MD32748 · Source: Drive, 29 Apr 2026

**Headline:** Which hybrids can still come in? · **Button:** Get quote · **Form:** Find-a-Hybrid form (below)
**Update rule:** if a model's approval expires, re-cut the 10–15 s line the same day.

### AD 2: "New price vs our price" · Carousel (5 cards) + 15 s Reel cut · Hook: Price anchor
**Job:** lead ad. Uses the strongest-spending hook (price anchor) with real numbers.

**Carousel, 1080×1080 each:**
1. Photo of a real C-HR Hybrid on the yard. Big text: **New: $[Toyota AU current drive-away price, C-HR Hybrid, from toyota.com.au on the day]** with a line through it. Under it: **Ours: $[cash price] · [year] · [km] km**
2. Same car, interior. **"Same hybrid system. [X] years younger than your current car."** (only if true for the buyer we're targeting; otherwise use **"Hybrid. Auto. Reverse camera."** with the real features)
3. The auction sheet photo. **"Graded in Japan before we bid. Grade [X]."**
4. Aureus detailing it. **"Prepped and detailed in Victoria Park."**
5. Navy end card. **"Tell us your budget. We'll find the hybrid."** · Licence MD32748 · 530 Albany Hwy

**Reel cut (15 s):** 0–2 s the price-strike card animates (a line through the new price) → 2–9 s a fast walk-around of the real car (cuts on movement: door opening, wheel, dash lighting up) → 9–13 s the "Ours: $[price]" card → 13–15 s end card.

**Rules:** the "new" price must be the real current Toyota Australia price for the closest equivalent model and grade, with the date in small print: *"New price: Toyota Australia, [date]."* The used car must show **year, rego, cash price and yard address** (WA dealer rules). No "save $X" claim unless the maths is exact.

**Caption:**
> New C-HR Hybrid: $[new price]. This one: $[our price]. 👀
> [Year] · [km] km · [colour] · Rego [XXX]
> Imported from Japan, graded before we bid, prepped in Victoria Park.
> Want something like it in your budget? Tap Get quote.
> Monsoor Motors · 530 Albany Hwy, Victoria Park · MV Dealership Licence MD32748

### AD 3: "I walk away from most cars" · Reel 25–30 s · Hook: Confession + founder
**Job:** trust. The ad for the cautious buyer who has heard import horror stories.

**Hook options:**
- A: *"Most cars I look at in Japan, I don't buy. Here's why."*
- B: *"This piece of paper decides whether I spend $[X] or walk away."*
- C: *"Confession: I've passed on cars that looked perfect."*

| Time | Picture | Monsoor says / on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Monsoor holding up a real auction sheet to camera (chassis no. blurred) | Hook. Text: **I WALK AWAY FROM MOST CARS** |
| 3–8 s | Close on the sheet, his finger on the grade | "Every car at a Japanese auction gets inspected and graded. This number is the grade." |
| 8–13 s | Finger moves to the body map | "This little map shows every scratch and dent. Letters and numbers, here, here." |
| 13–18 s | Cut to the real car: the same scratch spot, a 1-second close-up | "If the sheet says it's clean and the photos agree, we bid. If not, we walk." |
| 18–23 s | Hybrid battery / dash / service book, 1–2 s each | "On a hybrid we also look at the history and how it's been looked after." |
| 23–28 s | Monsoor, closer | "Want the sheet for the car you're looking at? I'll send it to you." |
| 28–30 s | End card | **Ask for the auction sheet** · *Learn more* |

**Caption:**
> Most cars we look at in Japan, we don't buy.
> Every import starts with the auction sheet: the grade, the kilometres, and a map of every scratch and dent. If it doesn't stack up, we walk away.
> Looking at a Japanese hybrid? Ask us for the sheet and we'll explain it, line by line.
> Monsoor Motors · Victoria Park · MV Dealership Licence MD32748

**Form:** "Send me the auction sheet for the car I want" (same form + a model field).

### AD 4: "Do hybrid batteries die?" · Reel 20–25 s · Hook: Myth busting / Warning
**Job:** removes the biggest objection. Also works as a strong organic post.

**Hook options:**
- A: *"'Hybrid batteries die at 100,000 km.' Let's talk about that."*
- B: *"Before you buy any used hybrid, ask this one question."*
- C: (text only) **"The hybrid battery question everyone asks us."**

| Time | Picture | Says / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Monsoor at the open bonnet of a hybrid | Hook |
| 3–9 s | Close-ups, 1–2 s each: hybrid system cover, dash hybrid display, service record | "Here's the honest answer: it depends on how the car's been treated, so check, don't guess." |
| 9–16 s | Monsoor counting on his fingers, cuts on each finger | Text appears one by one: **1. Service history · 2. Kilometres · 3. How it was checked before sale** |
| 16–21 s | Monsoor | "Ask any seller those three. If they can't answer, keep looking. Ask us, and we'll show you." |
| 21–24 s | End card | **Ask us about any hybrid** · *Send message* (Messenger/WhatsApp) |

**Rules:** no battery-life promises ("lasts forever", "never fails"). If we talk about warranty, it has to be the exact warranty we actually give.

**Caption:**
> "Hybrid batteries die at 100,000 km." Not that simple.
> Before you buy any used hybrid, ask: 1) service history, 2) kilometres, 3) how it was checked before sale.
> Ask us about any hybrid on our yard and we'll show you all three.
> Monsoor Motors · Victoria Park · MV Dealership Licence MD32748

### AD 5: "Just landed" · Reel 15–20 s + weekly Facebook Live · Hook: Newness
**Job:** stock ad. Runs every time a hybrid arrives. Retargeting and organic.

**5A Reel ("Japan to Victoria Park"):** no talking, ASMR and room sound.

| Time | Picture | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | The auction photo of the car (from the sheet) | **JUST LANDED 🇯🇵 → Perth** |
| 2–4 s | The same car arriving on the truck / yard | |
| 4–9 s | Aureus prep: foam (2 s), wipe (1 s), the bead of water (1 s), tyre shine (1 s) | |
| 9–14 s | Walk-around: door close, interior, hybrid display lights up | **[Year] [Model] Hybrid · [km] km** |
| 14–17 s | Hero shot, a slow push-in | **$[cash price] · Rego [XXX]** |
| 17–20 s | End card | **Be first to see it** · *Send message* |

Vary every shot length (no two shots the same length). Cut on movement. Hook rate target: 30%+.

**5B Facebook Live, "New arrivals walkaround", every Saturday 11 am, 10–15 min.**
Facebook Live gets **3× the interactions** of any other dealer format, and almost nobody does it. Monsoor walks the yard, opens each new hybrid, reads the sheet out loud and answers comments live ("What's the price on the white one?"). Afterwards, boost the best 30 s clip as an ad. Zero production cost.

### AD 6: "Trade up from the bowser" · Reel 15 s or two-card carousel · Hook: Contrast
**Job:** trade-in leads. Targets the real buyer (someone in an old petrol car, per importers quoted in Drive).

| Time | Picture | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Split screen. Left: an old petrol sedan at a Perth servo, the pump ticking up (real footage). Right: the hybrid's dash showing **[our test L/100 km]** | **YOUR CAR vs A HYBRID** |
| 3–8 s | The pump total climbing (real receipt close-up) | **Your fill: $[real receipt amount]** |
| 8–12 s | Monsoor hands over keys; the old car is parked to one side | **Bring your old car. We'll make a fair offer.** |
| 12–15 s | End card | **Free trade-in valuation** · *Get quote* |

**Fuel rule (ACCC):** only use (a) the **official rated figure** for that exact model, labelled "rated", or (b) **our own logged figure** ("Our test: [X] L/100 km over [Y] km of Perth driving"). Never "half the fuel", "eco-friendly" or "zero emissions".

**Form:** your car (make, model, year, km) + the hybrid you want + budget.

## Part 3: The lead form (use for Ads 1, 2, 3, 6)

**Type:** Higher intent (adds a review screen, so fewer junk leads).
**Intro:** "Tell us the hybrid you want. We'll call you with honest options. No obligation."

1. Which car? *(C-HR Hybrid · Corolla / Fielder Hybrid · Harrier Hybrid · Sienta Hybrid · Prius / Aqua · Not sure, help me choose)*
2. Budget? *(Under $20k · $20–30k · $30–40k · $40k+)*
3. When do you want it? *(Now · 1–3 months · Just looking)*
4. Got a car to trade in? *(Yes · No)*
5. Name · Mobile · Suburb

**Thank-you screen:** "Thanks, [name]. Monsoor or the team will call you within 15 minutes in business hours. 0434 137 393" · button: *Call now*
**Privacy:** link to the website privacy policy (Meta requires one).
**Call first:** "Now" + "Yes, trade-in" leads.

## Part 4: Test plan (week 1–2, inside the $20/day Leads campaign)

| Ad set | Ads in it | What we're testing |
|---|---|---|
| HYB – Leads (broad Perth) | Ad 1 (hooks A, B, C) · Ad 2 (carousel + reel) · Ad 6 | Which hook type wins: announcement, price anchor or contrast |
| HYB – Retarget ($5/day) | Ad 3 · Ad 4 · Ad 7 | Trust + objection + supply |

**Read the results at day 5–7:**
- Hook rate below 25% → change the first 3 seconds only.
- Hook rate fine but no leads → the offer or form is the problem.
- Winner = lowest **cost per called lead**, not cost per form.
- Week 3: put the winning hook style onto a new car (fresh creative every 3–4 weeks).
