## 1. The insight (why someone stops scrolling)

A Perth buyer who wants a C-HR Hybrid has two fears:
1. **"The door's closing."** Since Dec 2025 (ADR 79/05), most Japanese hybrids can't get *new* import approval.
2. **"I'll be buying a car I've never seen."**

The C-HR is one of the few still allowed in (see the truth checks in §7). So the story isn't "we sell cars". The story is:

> **Your C-HR is already sitting in Japan. It just doesn't have your name on it yet.**

The pre-order puts the buyer's name on that car, and the auction sheet in their hands before we bid.

## 2. The idea: one object carries the whole ad

**A blank paper tag on a covered car.** The tag reads *RESERVED FOR: ______*. The ad keeps coming back to that tag, and in the last second the blank becomes *you?*

- **Why a covered car:** it creates mystery (what's under there?). The AI never has to draw a real C-HR, so there are no morphing badges and no fake car. That keeps us inside the owner's rule: no AI pictures of our cars.
- **Why it's cheap:** *the content does the work, not the visuals.* The ad uses **3 visuals**. The text changes, a chat thread plays out and the tag fills in, so it feels like 12 shots.
- **Why text on screen:** in Virlo's study of 4,898 car videos (6 Oct–16 Nov 2026), 42.5% of car videos open on text on screen, and 30.3% of those beat twice the account's usual views. (Corrected 25 Nov: the best first frame is motion/action, at 41.5%.) The text also reads with the sound off.
- **Superseded:** on 25 Nov the owner preferred a humanised organic approach. See `CHR-Organic-Viral-Reels.md`.

> Setting: exterior, a Japanese vehicle export port at dawn. One car sits alone in the foreground under a fitted silver-grey car cover, rows of covered cars and stacked shipping containers soft in the background.
> Subject: the cream paper tag tied to the cover at the front, its printed words stay exactly as in the start image.
> Camera: handheld phone, slow dolly-in from a medium wide shot to a close-up of the tag over the whole shot, with slight natural breathing and one small reframe.
> Motion: the tag sways a little in the wind, the cover ripples gently, fine drizzle falls, a forklift crosses far in the background out of focus.
> Avoid: no people in the foreground, no logos, no badges, no number plates, no new text, the tag text must not change or morph.

The words "you?" on the tag are **added in the edit**, not by AI, so the text stays sharp and never morphs.

## 7. Truth checks (do these before anything goes live)

- [ ] **SEVS:** C-HR ZYX10 approval **SEV-000735** covers builds **Feb 2018–Aug 2019** and is listed as in force until **14 Dec 2027**. Two more approvals, SEV-000160 and SEV-000161, cover other build ranges. Confirm all three on ROVER. Pre-order only for build dates we can actually import. The "2019" in the chat must sit inside that range.
- [ ] **Deposit:** decide the amount and write the terms. It must be **fully refundable if we can't source the car**, with a clear time limit. Under the Australian Consumer Law (s36) we can't take payment for something we can't supply within a reasonable time.
- [ ] **The chat is a demonstration, not a customer.** It stays labelled *Example of how it works*, with no real names and no invented results. Swap in a real (blurred, permission-given) chat once one exists.
- [ ] **Auction sheet:** a real sheet from a real car, with the lot number, chassis number and auction house blurred.
- [ ] **V3:** if a caption says "this one landed in [month]", it must be true for that car. Otherwise keep the caption generic.
- [ ] **AI label:** V1 is a photorealistic AI scene. Switch on Meta's "AI info" label, or add a small *AI-generated scene* tag on V1.
- [ ] **Price (opening 3 only):** year, rego or VIN, cash price and dealer details on screen. No finance wording (Meta special category).
- [ ] "Rule change" line: say *"can't get new import approval"*, never *"banned"*.

## 8. The edit (CapCut is on the Mac. Premiere Pro isn't installed, but the same timeline works in either)

Tracks, from top to bottom:
1. **Text:** Montserrat ExtraBold, white with a soft shadow, inside 1080×1920 (keep the top 250 px and bottom 400 px clear). Watermark top left at about 55%.
2. **V2 overlay:** chat PNG/MOV with alpha (we render it free in Python, the same way as the storyboard).
3. **Video:** the V1 clip placed 4 times with different crops and speed 100% (never retimed), plus V3.
4. **VO** at −6 dB peak. **SFX** (rain, pops, stamp, whoosh, pen) at −18 dB. **Music** at about −19 dB, so about 13 dB under the VO.

Rules from CLAUDE.md:
- cut on movement (the tag swing, the cover pull)
- match the white balance of V1 (cold) to V3 (warmer) with a small warm-up, not a heavy grade
- no grain filter, zoom punches or flash cuts

Export H.264, 1080×1920, 30 fps, about 12 Mbps.
