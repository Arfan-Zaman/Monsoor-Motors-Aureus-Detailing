## 1. How their ad is built (the formula)

| Time | Their scene | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2.5 s | Founder **walking toward camera** across the workshop floor, branded polo, talking | Moving person = pattern interrupt. Talks to "you" straight away |
| 2.5–5 s | **Cutaway: shopfront sign** from the street | Proves they're real and local |
| 5–7.5 s | Back to founder, caption "Head quarters" | Ownership: "this is our place" |
| 7.5–10 s | **Animated map of Australia** | Simple visual for "import to Australia" |
| 10–15 s | **Workshop B-roll:** car on a hoist, a mechanic working | Proof of capability (the compliance work) |
| 15–34 s | **Car parade:** 7 cars, ~2.4 s each, **name label on each** (S2000, Crown Majesta, GTRs, Mercedes, S-Class, LS460, Subaru STI) | Shows range and "we've done cars like yours". The most watchable part |
| 34–36 s | Founder + a **flag/country sticker** pop-in | Keeps energy up |
| 36–43 s | Founder **gesturing to camera**, a label pops in big ("VAB STI") | Re-hook halfway through |
| 43–46 s | A **telephone graphic drops in** next to him | Visual CTA: "call us" |
| 46–50 s | Founder close, caption "your confusion" | The promise: we take the confusion away |

**Style notes:** a cut every **~2–3 s**. Founder always **moving and gesturing**, never standing still. **One short caption at a time** (2–4 words, white bold, mid-frame). Bright, busy workshop with a lot of cars visible. Sunglasses and a polo, casual not corporate. Phone-shot feel, not a film crew.

## 3. How UGC ads work, and how they'd work for us

**UGC (user-generated content)** = an ad that looks like a normal person's phone video, not a company ad: a customer, a creator or a staff member talking to their phone, filmed selfie-style. It works on Meta because it blends into the feed (people don't scroll past it as "an ad"), and because buyers trust other buyers more than dealers. Meta and agency guides put phone-shot UGC among the top formats for Reels ads, and Meta's system (Andromeda) treats each different person and setting as a **new concept**, so UGC is also the cheapest way to get the creative variety the algorithm wants.

### The four kinds of UGC we could use

| Type | Who | Cost | Use it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Real customer UGC** | A happy buyer films 20–30 s at handover or a week later | Free, or a thank-you (e.g. a free Aureus wash) | **Yes, the best one.** Most believable |
| **2. Staff as creator** | Arfan or a team member, phone in hand: "I work at a car yard, here's what people get wrong buying hybrids" | Free | **Yes.** Easy, unlimited, fresh faces |
| **3. Paid local creator** | A Perth lifestyle/car creator, run as a **Partnership ad** (shows their name + ours) | ~$150–500 per video (ask several) | Later, once ads are working |
| **4. AI avatar "customer"** | AI-generated person pretending to be a buyer | cheap | **No.** A fake customer = fake testimonial. Banned under the Australian Consumer Law and our own CLAUDE.md rules |

### Rules for UGC (ACCC + Meta)
- **Real customers only, saying what really happened.** No scripts that put words in their mouth. We can give them prompts (questions), not lines.
- If they got **anything in return** (free detail, discount, money), it has to be disclosed, and the thank-you can't depend on them saying something positive: "Thanks to [name], who received a free detail for sharing this." Paid creators → use Meta's **Paid partnership** label.
- Written permission to use their face and video in ads (a one-line consent form or text message is fine; keep it on file).
- Blur number plates. Don't show their address or anything personal.
- Fuel or savings claims in their words must still be true ("I used to spend about $X a week, now about $Y": their own real numbers).

## 4. Three UGC scripts (prompts, not lines)

### UGC 1: "Handover selfie" (customer, 20–30 s, filmed by the customer or a staff member on their phone)
Ask them these on the spot, and film the answers, selfie-style beside their new hybrid:
1. "What were you driving before?"
2. "Why a hybrid?"
3. "What was it like buying from us?"
4. "Say hi to the car." (the fun ending)
**Edit:** cut the best 3 answers, captions on, a 2 s end card. **Hook:** their first sentence (e.g. "I was driving a 2006 Falcon…").

### UGC 2: "One week later" (customer, 20 s)
Text them 7 days after purchase: "Would you film a quick 20-second video in the car about your first week? Here are 3 questions: What surprised you? How's the fuel? Would you tell a mate?"
**Hook options:** their fuel receipt held to the camera, or the hybrid display showing their real figure.

### UGC 3: "Car yard insider" (staff as creator: Arfan, 25–30 s, selfie-walk around the yard)
- **Hook:** "I work at a car yard in Perth. Three questions to ask before you buy any used hybrid."
- 1) "Ask for the service history." (shows a service book) 2) "Ask how the hybrid battery was checked." (bonnet) 3) "If it's an import, ask for the auction sheet." (sheet)
- **End:** "That's what we do at Monsoor Motors in Victoria Park. Ask us anything." + licence on the end card.

## 5. How they all fit into the Meta campaign

One campaign, one broad Perth ad set ($20/day), mixing **different people and formats** so Andromeda finds different buyers:

1. **Clone ad** (Monsoor walk-and-talk, §2): hook test with 3 openings
2. **UGC 3**: Arfan "car yard insider"
3. **UGC 1**: first real customer handover (as soon as we have one)
4. **Ad 2 carousel**: new price vs our price
5. **Ad 3**: auction sheet confession

Every 2–3 weeks, add a new customer UGC. **Real customers are the creative engine**: each sale makes the next ad.
