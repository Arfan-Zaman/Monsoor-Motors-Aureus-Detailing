# Japanese Hybrid Imports: Meta Ad Plan (Facebook + Instagram)

**For:** Monsoor Motors · **Written:** 24 Nov 2026 · **Status:** Draft for owner approval
**Cars:** Toyota C-HR Hybrid, Sienta Hybrid, Corolla Axio / Fielder Hybrid, Harrier Hybrid, Prius, Aqua and similar fuel-efficient Japanese hybrids

---

## 1. The short answer: is it worth it?

**Yes, but not the way you were planning.** Demand is strong. Supply from Japan is closing.

**Why demand is good**
- Used hybrids are selling fast. In June 2026, CommBank reported that hybrids made up 1 in 10 sales of used cars up to five years old, and that used hybrids kept 93.4% of their value.
- Fuel is expensive (about $2.37/L across the big cities in October 2026), and buyers are moving to hybrids because of it.
- The typical import buyer is upgrading from an old petrol car (a 15–20-year-old Commodore or Falcon), not trading in a new car. That person scrolls Facebook and Marketplace, so Meta is the right place.

**The big risk you need to know before spending a dollar**
- From **1 December 2025**, a new emissions rule (ADR 79/05, the Euro 6d level) stopped new SEVS approvals for most fuel-efficient Japanese-market cars. The Japanese standard is not accepted as equivalent.
- Models approved **before** December 2025 can still be imported, but only until their SEVS entry expires (entries last up to 3 years) and **they can't be renewed**.
- Drive (29 Apr 2026) reported the current **Toyota Prius** entry has **already expired** (1 Dec 2025). **Sienta Hybrid** and Alphard/Vellfire entries were due to expire around May 2026. Others run out over the next 3 years.
- Cars already in Australia and complied are **not affected**. They can be bought, sold and registered as normal.

**So the smart play is:**
1. **Sell the window, honestly.** "Japanese hybrids are getting harder to bring in" is true, newsworthy and a strong reason to enquire now. It isn't a fake "hurry, sale ends Sunday".
2. **Only advertise "we'll import it for you" for models whose SEVS entry is still current.** Check each one first (see section 2).
3. **Start buying hybrids locally as well.** As imports dry up, used hybrids already here go up in value. Run a "we buy hybrids" ad next to the selling ads so you keep stock coming in.

> **Verdict:** a small, steady budget (about **$25 a day**, around **$750 a month**) is worth it if **one extra car a month** comes from the ads. At normal used-import margins, one car pays for the month several times over. See section 4.

## 3. Who we target

**Main buyer:** Perth, age 25–60, driving an older petrol car, worried about fuel, wants something reliable that "feels new" for $18k–$35k. Often a family (Sienta 7-seat, Fielder wagon), a commuter (Prius, Axio, Aqua) or someone who wants a stylish SUV (C-HR, Harrier).

**Second buyer:** rideshare and delivery drivers. Fuel is their biggest cost, so the savings sell themselves.

**Third audience (for the buying side):** people who already own a hybrid and might sell or trade it.

**Meta settings (keep it simple, let Meta find buyers)**
- Location: Perth metro + 60 km (add Mandurah, Bunbury and Joondalup later if leads are good). Target **people living in** the area, not just passing through.
- Age 25–65, all genders. Use **Advantage+ audience** (broad). Don't stack interests, because small budgets work better broad.
- Placements: Advantage+ (Feed, Reels, Stories, Marketplace). **Marketplace placement matters for cars.**
- No special ad category for the car ads themselves. If an ad mentions loans or repayments, it becomes a Financial products ad, so don't mention them.

## 4. Budget and the maths (why it's sustainable)

### Budget: start small, earn the right to grow

| Phase | Weeks | Daily | Monthly | What runs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Test** | 1–4 | $25 | ~$750 | Lead form campaign ($20) + retargeting ($5) |
| **Keep** | 5–8 | $25–35 | ~$750–1,050 | Kill losers, put money behind the 2 best ads |
| **Grow** | 9+ | $40–60 | ~$1,200–1,800 | Only if cost per sale (below) is under target |

Never increase the budget by more than **about 20% every 3–4 days**. Bigger jumps reset Meta's learning.

### What to expect (planning numbers, check against our own results)

| Number | Planning range | Source / note |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per lead (form filled) | **$30–$60** | Automotive Meta lead benchmarks run about US$25–50, and up to ~US$80 for form leads. Australia tracks close to global |
| Leads per month at $750 | **~12–25** | |
| Lead → sale | **4–8%** for form leads; higher for Messenger/phone | Depends almost entirely on how fast we call |
| Sales per month from ads | **~1–2** | |

### The rule that keeps it sustainable

- **Most we'll pay per sale = 25% of gross profit on that car.** Example: if a hybrid makes $3,500 gross, we can spend up to ~$875 in ads to sell it.
- At $750 a month, **one sale a month covers it.** Two sales and it's clearly working, so grow.
- **Cost per sale = ad spend ÷ cars sold from ad leads.** Track it weekly in a simple sheet (section 8).

## 5. The ads (6 concepts, show-don't-tell)

Brand rules apply to every ad: real cars, real photos, daylight, plates blurred, Montserrat, navy #0F2747 with orange #FF5F15 as an accent only, logo top-left. Captions are honest and local, never "best deal in Perth". **No AI images of the cars** (owner rule; Meta also labels realistic AI images). Video voice is Arfan's locked voice track, or Monsoor on camera.

**Legal lines on every ad (WA Motor Vehicle Dealers Act):** "Monsoor Motors · MV Dealership Licence MD32748". Any ad showing a **specific used car** must also show its **year, registration number, cash price and the yard address (530 Albany Hwy, Victoria Park)**. Put these in the caption or on the image.

---

### Ad 1: "The rules just changed" (reel, 30–40 s): **the hook ad**
*Goal: lead form. This is the ad most likely to win, because it's real news nobody else in Perth is explaining.*

- **0–3 s:** Monsoor at the yard, beside a C-HR Hybrid, to camera: *"If you've been thinking about a Japanese hybrid, there's something you need to know."* On-screen text: **Japanese hybrid imports are closing.**
- **3–20 s:** Plain explanation, cutting between the car, an auction sheet and the yard: since December, Australia's new emissions rule means many Japanese hybrids can't get new import approval. Some already can't come in, and others have a set end date. Cars already here are fine.
- **20–30 s:** *"We can still bring in some models, for now. Tell us what you're after and we'll tell you straight whether it's still possible."*
- **CTA button:** *Get Quote* → instant form.
- **Rules:** say only what's true on the day it's posted. Show the source ("Source: Drive, Apr 2026") in the caption. No made-up deadlines. Update the ad the moment a model drops off.

### Ad 2: "Find Me a Hybrid" (static + carousel): **the workhorse lead ad**
*Goal: lead form. Runs all the time.*

- **Image:** a real C-HR / Harrier / Fielder on the yard. Headline: **"Tell us the hybrid. We'll find it."** Sub-line: *Hand-picked in Japan or here in Perth · Inspected · Ready to register in WA*
- **Carousel version:** one card per model (C-HR, Harrier, Corolla Fielder, Aqua). Each card shows a real photo and *"from $X drive-away"* **only if we can stand behind the price**. Otherwise just *"Ask for today's price"*.
- **Form (higher-intent type, with an extra review screen):** Which car? · Budget (under $20k / $20–30k / $30–40k / $40k+) · When do you want it? (now / 1–3 months / just looking) · Got a car to trade? (yes/no) · Name, mobile, suburb.
  The "when" and "trade" answers tell us who to call first.

### Ad 3: "We read this before we bid" (carousel): **trust**
*Goal: lead form + retargeting. Shows the expertise that makes an import safe.*

- Card 1: a real Japanese **auction sheet** (blur the chassis number). **"Every import starts here."**
- Cards 2–4: three things we check, each with a close-up photo and one line: grade and kilometres · body map (scratches and dents marked) · hybrid battery and service history.
- Card 5: the same car, detailed and on the yard. **"If the sheet doesn't stack up, we walk away."**
- CTA: *Learn more* → form: "Send me the auction sheet for the car I want."

### Ad 4: "Your old car → a hybrid" (static pair / short reel): **trade-in upgrade**
*Goal: lead form. Targets the real buyer (someone in an old petrol car).*

- Split image: an older petrol sedan at the bowser on the left, a C-HR Hybrid on the yard on the right. Headline: **"Trade up from the bowser."** Sub-line: *Bring your old car. We'll give you a fair offer and put you in a hybrid.*
- **Fuel numbers rule:** only use **official rated figures** for the exact model, labelled as "rated", or the **real logged figures** from our own car ("We drove ours for a week: X L/100 km"). Never "half the fuel" or "eco-friendly" without proof (ACCC green-claim guidance).
- Form: rego and km of your current car + which hybrid you want.

### Ad 5: "Japan to Victoria Park" (reel, 25–35 s): **story**
*Goal: video views, then retargeting into Ads 2 and 3. This builds the brand.*

- Real footage only, varied shot lengths, cut on movement (CLAUDE.md editing rules): auction photo → car arriving → compliance workshop → **Aureus Detailing** prep (foam, wipe, the shine) → key handover on the yard.
- No voiceover needed: room tone, the detailing ASMR and one line of text at the end: **"Hand-picked in Japan. Ready in Perth."** End card: logo, 0434 137 393, licence MD32748.
- Cross-sells Aureus at no extra cost.

### Ad 6: "We buy hybrids" (static): **the supply side**
*Goal: lead form from sellers. Keeps stock coming in as imports dry up.*

- Image: a hybrid being inspected on the yard. Headline: **"Selling your hybrid? We're buying."** Sub-line: *Fair offer, paid fast, we handle the paperwork.*
- Form: make, model, year, km, suburb, photos via WhatsApp after the call.
- Budget: $5/day from week 3, or more if stock is short.

### Retargeting (the $5/day)
Show **Ads 3 and 6, plus a happy-customer handover photo** (customer permission, plate blurred) to people who watched 50%+ of Ad 1 or Ad 5, opened a form and didn't finish, or engaged with the Page or Instagram in the last 30 days.

## 6. Campaign setup (Meta Ads Manager)

| Campaign | Objective | Budget | Ad sets | Ads |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HYB – Leads** | Leads → Instant form (higher intent) | $20/day (CBO) | 1 broad Perth ad set | Ad 1, Ad 2 (static + carousel), Ad 4 |
| **HYB – Retarget** | Leads → Instant form | $5/day | Video viewers 50% + form openers + engagers 30d | Ad 3, Ad 6, customer handover |
| **HYB – Story** (from week 3) | Engagement / ThruPlay | $5/day, taken from Leads if needed | Broad Perth | Ad 5 |

- Turn on **Messenger and WhatsApp** as contact options. Some buyers won't fill in forms but will message.
- Connect leads to a **Google Sheet or the Meta Leads Center** with an instant SMS or email alert to the person answering leads.
- Once 5–10 sales have come from ads, upload sold leads back to Meta (**Conversions API / offline events**) so it learns who actually buys, not just who fills in forms. This is the single biggest long-term improvement.

## 7. Turning leads into sales (where the money is actually made)

1. **Call within 15 minutes.** If there's no answer, SMS right away:
   > *Hi [name], Monsoor Motors here, thanks for asking about a [car]. I've got a couple of options in your budget. Is now a good time for a quick call? – [name], 0434 137 393*
2. **On the call:** confirm car, budget, timing and trade-in. Then **send something real within the hour**: a car on the yard, an auction-sheet example or a price range for an import.
3. **Book a yard visit or test drive.** That's the goal of every call.
4. **Follow-up:** Day 1 call + SMS → Day 2 a matching car (photo/video) → Day 4 call → Day 7 "still looking?" SMS → then monthly with new stock.
5. Log every lead: **source ad · date · called (Y/N) · visited · sold · gross**.

## 8. Measuring, plus kill and scale rules

**Weekly check (10 minutes, every Monday):** spend · leads · cost per lead · calls made · visits · sales · cost per sale.

| Signal | Rule |
|---|---|
| An ad has spent **$60+ with 0 leads** | Pause it |
| Cost per lead above **$80** for 7 days | Change the hook (first 3 s / headline). Don't touch targeting first |
| Lots of leads but **no one answers or buys** | The problem is lead quality or follow-up: make the form stricter (higher intent, add the "when" question) and check call speed |
| Cost per sale under **25% of gross**, two weeks running | Raise the budget by 20% |
| A model's SEVS entry expires | Remove "we can import it" from every ad for that model **the same day** |

New creative every **3–4 weeks** (new car, new hook). Small budgets tire out the same ad fast.
