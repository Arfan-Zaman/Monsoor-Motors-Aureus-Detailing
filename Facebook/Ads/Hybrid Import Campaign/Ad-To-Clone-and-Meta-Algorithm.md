| **UniBee Australia** (Dandenong VIC, hybrid importer) | Stock/catalogue ads with price, plus an offer ad (5-yr warranty, roadside, full tank), plus "Sick of paying through the nose for fuel?" | Many since **9 Oct 2026** | ~46 days and still paying, so their **stock ads with price + warranty stack** are their proven format |

### What AutoLinx's 6 ads tell us (they're paying to learn this, we get it free)
They test **one structure with six different openings**:
1. **Local call-out:** "Perth, looking to import a car from Japan?" ← **most impressions**
2. **Humour:** "your missus finally gave you permission… don't screw this up"
3. **Fear:** "spending thousands on a car you've never seen in person"
4. **Biggest mistake:** "looks perfect in photos, a nightmare when it arrives"
5. **Problem/rescue:** "stuck trying to get it complied?"
6. **Plain intro:** "we specialise in importing cars from Japan"

Every one then runs the same body: **the problem → "we handle everything" → proof (500+ cars imported, 4.9★, 75+ Google reviews) → book a call.**

Their gap: they sell **JDM performance cars, 4x4s, GTRs and kei trucks**, not hybrids. They don't mention fuel, the hybrid rule change or cars on a yard you can drive today. **The hybrid buyer is unclaimed in Perth.**

## 2. The one to clone: AutoLinx's "Perth call-out" founder video, rebuilt for hybrids

**Why this one**
- Same city, same buyer, still live, and it gets the most impressions of their set.
- A founder on camera is the format Meta's own research rewards: brand and message in the first 5 s makes an ad **1.7×** more likely to reach the top purchase-intent group.
- It's cheap: one person, one phone, one morning.
- We stack **UniBee's proven stuff** into it (fuel pain + a clear offer stack) and **our own edge** (hybrids, the rule change, stock on the yard now).

### Our version: "Perth, still want a Japanese hybrid?" (Reel, 35–40 s, Monsoor to camera)

| Time | Picture | Monsoor says (our words) / on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Monsoor walking toward camera between two hybrids on the yard | **"Perth, if you've been thinking about a Japanese hybrid, listen to this."** Text: **PERTH · JAPANESE HYBRIDS** |
| 3–9 s | Close on the fuel gauge, then a quick cut to a Perth servo price board | "Fuel's expensive, and everyone wants a hybrid. But the government changed the import rules in December." |
| 9–15 s | Auction sheet in hand, then the Drive headline on a phone | "A lot of Japanese hybrids can't get new approval now. Some already can't come in." Text: *Source: Drive, Apr 2026* |
| 15–22 s | Walk-around of a real hybrid, door close, the dash lighting up | "So here's how we do it. Cars that are already here, inspected and detailed. Or, while we still can, we import the one you want, and you get the auction sheet first." |
| 22–30 s | Monsoor, closer, a small push-in | **Proof (real numbers only):** "[X] cars sold · [X]★ Google rating from [X] reviews · licensed dealer in Victoria Park." |
| 30–36 s | End card | **Tell us the hybrid you want** · *Get quote* · 0434 137 393 · MV Dealership Licence MD32748 |

**Headline:** Perth's Japanese hybrid people · **Button:** Get quote → Find-a-Hybrid instant form (Higher intent)

### Film 5 different openings (that's what makes Meta's algorithm work, see section 3)
Same body (3–36 s), with **five genuinely different first 3 seconds**:
1. **Local call-out:** "Perth, if you've been thinking about a Japanese hybrid…" (the clone)
2. **Fuel pain** (UniBee's angle, our words): Monsoor at the pump, the total ticking up: "Filling up every week hurts. Here's the fix."
3. **Biggest mistake:** "The biggest mistake buying a cheap hybrid? Not asking about the battery."
4. **Warning / news:** "If you want an imported hybrid, the clock's ticking. Here's why."
5. **Humour** (Monsoor's style): wife or kid in the passenger seat holding up a fuel receipt, then deadpan to camera: "She told me to fix this."

## 3. How Meta's ad algorithm works now, and what we do about it

### The three parts (from Meta's own engineering blog)
1. **Andromeda (retrieval):** from millions of ads, picks the small set worth considering for each person. It reads the **ad itself** (video, words, image) to decide who it's for. **Your creative is now your targeting.**
2. **GEM (Meta's ads "brain"):** a large AI model that predicts who will act. Meta reports it lifted conversions **18.9%** and click-through **12%** across its apps (2026).
3. **Lattice (ranking):** learns across all ads and objectives and shares what works.

### What that means for a $25/day budget

| Do this | Why |
|---|---|
| **Broad targeting** (Perth + 60 km, 25–65, Advantage+ audience, no interest stacking) | Andromeda finds the buyer from the creative. Narrow targeting just starves it |
| **5–8 genuinely different ads** (different hook, person, setting or angle, like the 5 openings above) | Near-copies (a colour swap, a new headline) get treated as the same ad and add nothing. Different concepts reach different pockets of people. This is exactly what AutoLinx is doing |
| **One campaign, one ad set** | Small budgets can't feed several ad sets. Meta's learning phase wants ~50 results per ad set per week. At ~$25/day we'll get roughly 4–8 leads a week, so **"Learning limited" is normal; ignore it**. Just don't split the money |
| **Don't touch it for 5–7 days** after launch or after a big change | Every edit restarts learning |
| **Raise budget max ~20% at a time** | Bigger jumps reset learning |
| **Sound off, captions on, brand early** | Meta: brand + message in the first 5 s → 1.7×; brand across several scenes → 1.8× |
| **Hook rate (3-s views ÷ impressions) ≥ 30%** | Meta uses early attention as a quality signal |
| **Fresh concept every 3–4 weeks** | Small audiences tire of an ad fast. Watch for frequency above ~3 |

### The upgrade that matters most: teach Meta who actually BUYS
- Normal lead ads optimise for **"filled in a form"**, so Meta finds form-fillers, including tyre-kickers.
- **Conversion Leads** optimisation tells Meta which leads became **calls, test drives and sales**, using the Conversions API for CRM. Meta reports about **21% lower cost per quality lead** than the standard Leads goal. Since April 2026 (per a secondary source), the "qualified leads" goal needs this connection.
- **How we do it simply:**
  1. Leads go into a Google Sheet (via Make or Zapier).
  2. The team marks each lead: *Called → Visited → Sold*.
  3. Make sends those stages back to Meta.
  4. Once we have steady volume, switch the campaign goal to **Conversion leads**.
- Meta's rule: the stage we optimise for should convert **1–40%** of leads. So optimise for **"Visited / test drive"** first (not "Sold", which is too rare at our volume).
- Meta says setup to full optimisation can take a few weeks. **Start collecting the stages from day 1**, even before the connection is live.
