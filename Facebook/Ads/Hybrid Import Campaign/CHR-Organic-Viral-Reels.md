Breakout videos ran a **median 39 s**, against 34 s for all car videos. Organic posts can run longer than the 29 s paid cut.

**What this means for us:**
- **Don't open on Monsoor talking to camera.** It's the weakest first frame. Open on something moving (a hand, a foam cannon, a cover coming off), then let him talk.
- **Tell a story, or show a transformation.** Storytime and before-and-after are the two formats that break out most often.
- **Start with a shock line or a story tease,** never "Hi, we're Monsoor Motors".

## 2. Five C-HR Reels, each built on a format that has worked

Film all five in **one morning** with one phone. Post one every 2–3 days.

---

### Reel 1: "What a C-HR Hybrid *really* costs to land in Perth" (Fukasawa + Rohrman format)
**Format:** explainer with a price reveal · **Hook:** shock statement · **Length:** 35–40 s

- **0–2 s, first frame:** a hand slaps the real auction sheet onto the C-HR's bonnet. Text: **"This C-HR cost $[X] in Japan."**
- **2–4 s:** Monsoor, off-camera, dry: *"By the time it's in Perth? Watch."*
- **4–30 s:** one item per beat. A calculator or notepad on the bonnet, with each number written in by hand. Real numbers only:
  - auction price
  - auction and agent fees
  - shipping
  - import duty
  - GST
  - compliance
  - inspection
  - rego
  - Aureus detail
  - **running total**
- **30–36 s:** Monsoor leans in to camera, for the first time: *"Now you know why some dealers won't show you this."*
- **36–39 s:** the C-HR's door closes. Text: *"Want one? Comment C-HR."*

**Why it spreads:** people argue about the numbers in the comments ("you can do it cheaper!"), and every comment pushes it to more people. Reply to every comment with a short video reply. That's a free second Reel.

---

### Reel 2: "From Japan dust to Victoria Park" (before and after, run by Aureus)
**Format:** before and after · **Hook:** motion first frame · **Length:** 30–45 s, mostly no talking

- **First frame:** a finger drags through real dust on the just-landed C-HR (from Japan or the shipping yard). Text: **"Straight off the boat from Japan."**
- The Aureus detailer works: snow foam, wheel brush, a mitt pass, a clay bar, the interior vacuum, the hybrid badge wiped.
- **Real close-mic sound only:** hiss, scrub, drip. Shot lengths vary (1–4 s), and each cut lands on a wipe or a spray.
- **Payoff:** a slow walk-around in the late-afternoon sun, then a key handed to camera. Text: **"Ready for its Perth owner. Could be you."**
- Caption: model and year, "imported and detailed in-house", and a comment prompt.

**Why:** before and after hits 35%, and silent aesthetic videos have the biggest jumps (5.6×). We own a detailing business, so nobody else in Perth can make this every week for free.

---

### Reel 3: "A customer asked me to find a C-HR in Japan…" (storytime series)
**Format:** storytime, in parts · **Hook:** story tease · **Length:** 30–40 s each

- **First frame:** Monsoor scrolling the Japanese auction screen on a laptop, his finger on the trackpad. Text: **"Part 1: a customer gave me $[X]k and one rule."**
- Monsoor, half to camera, half to the screen: *"White. Under 60,000 km. No scratches on the bumper. I've got four days."*
- Show the real search: three candidates, then one rejected because of a bad grade on its auction sheet (zoom on the grade). *"This one looks perfect. Look at the sheet."*
- **End on a cliffhanger:** *"Auction's Thursday. Part 2 if we win."*
- **Parts 2–4:** the bid result (real), the ship, the arrival, and **the customer's real reaction at the handover** (filmed with their written permission).

**Why:** storytime has the best hit rate of the big formats (39%). A series makes people follow to see the ending, and following builds the account.
**Truth rule:** only with a real customer and a real search. No customer yet? Use Reel 4 instead, or tell the story of the black C-HR on our yard (how we found and bought it).

---

### Reel 4: "3 things to check before you buy an imported hybrid" (insider tip)
**Format:** tutorial · **Hook:** question · **Length:** 30–35 s

- **First frame:** a torch clicks on, aimed into the C-HR's engine bay. Text: **"Buying an imported hybrid? Check these 3 first."**
1. **The auction sheet grade and map.** Show the real sheet and what the codes mean (A1 = small scratch, and so on).
2. **The hybrid system health check.** Show the scan tool or the battery report on screen.
3. **The compliance plate and the build date.** Point at it: *"If the date's wrong, it can't be registered."*
- **End:** *"We do all three before you see the car. Save this for when you buy."*

**Why:** tutorials and saves travel far, and the buyer feels we're on their side. Every claim here must match what we really do.

---

### Reel 5: "Australia quietly blocked most Japanese hybrids" (news with a shock statement)
**Format:** explainer · **Hook:** shock statement · **Length:** 25–30 s

- **First frame:** a phone held up, the Drive headline scrolling. Text: **"Most Japanese hybrids can't be imported any more."**
- Monsoor, walking along a row of cars on our yard: *"New rule, December 2025. A lot of models can't get new approval. Some C-HRs still can, for now."*
- Cut to the C-HR: *"So if you've wanted one, here's what that means for you…"* (cars already here are fine, a pre-order window, prices could rise. Say "could"; never promise).
- End: *"Comment HYBRID and I'll tell you if yours can still come in."*

**Wording:** say "can't get new import approval", never "banned". Check each model's status on ROVER first.

### Bonus: the Aspley Mazda move (staff personality, 0 cars needed)
Once a fortnight, post a fun clip with Monsoor and the Aureus team: a trend sound, a skit about "customers when they see the fuel price", or Monsoor's locked "Good… good." laugh when a car sells. **It sells the people, not the car.** People buy from dealers they feel they know.

## 3. Posting playbook

- **Which accounts:** post all five on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. Upload the clean file each time, because watermarked reposts get pushed down.
- **When:** 3 posts a week. Tue, Thu and Sat between 5 and 8:30 pm Perth time, and check Insights after 2 weeks.
- **Captions:** burned into the video, centre safe area, Montserrat. Most people watch with the sound off.
- **Sound:** our own voice and room sound first. If music, use the platform's licensed library, quietly underneath.
- **Comment prompt on every post** ("comment C-HR") and reply within an hour. Video replies to the best comments.
- **Boosting:** after 48 h, any Reel at 2× our usual views gets $10–20/day as a Meta ad (Perth +60 km) with the Get quote form. Organic finds the winner, and paid scales it.
- **Track** in a sheet: views, 3-s hold %, average watch time, shares, saves, comments, DMs and enquiries.

## 5. Turning views into C-HR pre-orders

**The offer, "The Japan List":** one auction run a month for C-HR pre-orders.
- A $[X] deposit holds your place. It is **fully refundable** if you don't approve a car, or if we don't find one within [60] days.
- You approve every car from its auction sheet before we bid.
- The list closes on a real date each month. That's honest urgency, never fake scarcity.

**The path:** Reel → comment "C-HR" → an automatic DM → 4 questions (year, colour, max km, budget) → Monsoor calls within 15 minutes → deposit → the auction.
- Set up the comment-to-DM auto-reply in Meta Business Suite, or ManyChat.
- Pin a 20 s "How The Japan List works" Reel to the top of the profile.

**What each Reel adds to the pre-order:**
| Reel | Ends with | Moves people to pre-order by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Cost breakdown | "Comment C-HR and I'll do this breakdown for your car" | The reply is a personalised landed quote, which starts the pre-order |
| 2 Before and after | "This one's taken. The Japan List closes [date]" | Shows the end result they're buying |
| 3 Storytime | Each real pre-order customer becomes a series (with permission) | Viewers follow someone else's car and want their own |
| 4 Buyer tips | "Save this" | Retarget the people who saved it with the Japan List ad |
| 5 Rule news | "Comment HYBRID, I'll tell you if yours can still come in" | Honest urgency while the approval lasts |

**Paid support:** boost any Reel at 2× usual views ($10–20/day, Get quote form). Retarget people who watched 50%+ of any Reel with the pinned "How it works" Reel.
**Track:** views → comments → DMs → calls → deposits.
**Before launch:** the deposit amount, written refund terms, and a check on ROVER of which C-HR build dates can still come in.
