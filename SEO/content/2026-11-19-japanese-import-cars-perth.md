## The blog post

# Japanese Import Cars in Perth

Looking for a Japanese import car in Perth? Start with the car you actually want, not with a long list of models you have never heard of.

At Monsoor Motors in Victoria Park, we hand-pick Japanese imports — quality used cars and Japanese hybrid imports, inspected and ready — and we also find cars to order through our Find Me a Car service. Tell us the car you want, and we will find it, locally or at Japanese auction.

## Why Japanese imports work in Perth

Japanese cars are built for reliability, and a lot of them come with higher spec than the same model sold here. Hybrids are a good example. A Toyota Aqua, Prius C, Crown Hybrid or Alphard Hybrid can give you low running costs and a well-kept car, because Japanese cars are usually serviced on time and driven less hard than you might expect.

That does not mean every import is a good buy. The difference is in the check before it lands here, and the check after it arrives.

## How we pick an import

We do not buy blind. A good import starts with the auction sheet, the service history, and a clear read on kilometres, condition and grade. If the sheet does not stack up, we walk away.

Once a car is here, it is inspected and prepared before it goes on sale. If we cannot stand behind it, we do not sell it.

That is the short version. If you want the detail, ask us about a specific car and we will talk you through its history, not just its price.

## What to check before you buy any Japanese import

- **Kilometres and history:** low kilometres help, but service history matters more. Ask for it.
- **Hybrid battery:** on a hybrid, ask how it was checked. A cheap hybrid with a tired battery is not cheap.
- **Compliance and roadworthy:** make sure the car meets Australian standards and is ready to register in WA. Ask, do not assume.
- **Drive-away price:** ask what is included. Our listings show prices as exc. gov. charges, so check the drive-away figure before you decide.
- **Finance:** finance is available on our cars. Ask what that looks like for the car you want, and what you need to bring.

If a seller cannot answer those five things clearly, keep looking.

## Can't find the one you want in stock?

That is normal with imports. Stock changes fast, and the exact car you want might not be on the yard today.

Tell us the car you want — make, model, year, budget, and what you will use it for — and we will find it, locally or at Japanese auction. That is our Find Me a Car service, and it is how a lot of our import customers end up with the right car instead of the closest one.

## Come and see them in Victoria Park

We have 20 vehicles on the yard at the time of writing, from Toyota, Honda, Mazda, Mitsubishi, Hyundai, Kia and more. The best way to judge an import is to sit in it, drive it, and ask questions while you are here.
