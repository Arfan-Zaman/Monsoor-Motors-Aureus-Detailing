- **Hustler with heart.** Hard-working, hands-on, not above any job. Talks like someone who has done the work himself.

- **Warm energy.** Calm and friendly at the start, builds to strong, confident energy on the promise. Smiles easily.
- **Australian with a mixed accent.** Light British accent blended with a relaxed Australian tone; says "mates".
- **Engineer's mind.** Explains things clearly, likes a checked, step-by-step process.
