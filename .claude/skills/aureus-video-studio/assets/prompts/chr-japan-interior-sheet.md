## Prompt

```text
Use @image1, @image2 and @image3 as strict photographic references of the interior of ONE real car: a right-hand-drive Toyota C-HR Hybrid (Japanese-market, first generation). Create a high-resolution studio-lit reference sheet of the EXACT SAME cabin. Not a redesign: it must look like this exact cabin, freshly detailed for sale, photographed professionally.

GLOBAL IDENTITY LOCK (CRITICAL). Preserve exactly what the references show:
- right-hand drive layout; the dashboard shape, the free-standing centre touchscreen, the diamond-shaped air vents, the hazard button, the climate control panel and its buttons, exactly as in @image1 and @image2
- seats: black fabric centres with a diamond-quilted pattern and light grey contrast stitching, grey leather-look side bolsters and edges, exactly as in @image2
- door trim: black textured door card with the diamond-pattern panel, piano-black window switch panel, chrome interior door handle (@image2)
- centre console: piano-black surround, chrome-topped gear lever with a black leather boot, the electronic parking brake and drive-mode buttons, cup holders (@image2)
- steering wheel: black leather Toyota wheel with the Toyota emblem, piano-black spoke trim, the left-spoke audio and MODE controls and the phone and voice buttons exactly as in @image3; the analogue hybrid instrument cluster behind it (@image1, @image3)
- keep every button symbol, label and screen exactly as photographed. Do not invent any text, logo or control.

PRE-SALE DETAILED CONDITION: vacuumed carpets and mats, wiped dust-free dash and piano-black trim with no fingerprints or smears, clean glass, clean seats. Remove loose items such as the cable on the floor. Keep the real leather grain and the real age of the materials: do not make anything look brand new or plastic, and do not change any material, colour or part.

REFERENCE SHEET LAYOUT (16:9, clean grid, no labels or text):
Large panel: the full dashboard seen from behind the front seats (right-hand drive).
Smaller panels: the driver's seat and passenger seat side by side; a close-up of the quilted seat fabric and stitching; the centre console with the gear lever; the steering wheel controls and instrument cluster; the door card and window switches.

LIGHTING: soft, even, neutral studio light inside the cabin, true colour, no harsh hot spots, no coloured light.

REALISM: real automotive interior photography, not CGI. Real fabric weave, real leather grain, real plastic texture, crisp stitching.

CAMERA: Canon EOS R5, RF 15-35mm f/8 for wide panels, RF 100mm f/2.8L Macro for close-ups, ISO 100, edge-to-edge sharpness.

FINAL RESULT: an ultra-realistic, perfectly sharp 4K reference sheet of this exact C-HR Hybrid cabin, detailed for sale.
```
