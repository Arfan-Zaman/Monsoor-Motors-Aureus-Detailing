## Camera, lighting and editing

- Framing: shot size medium close-up 80%, medium shot 20%; subject is a face (shot size from face height)
- Camera: moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 100%; typical move pan / track
- Lighting: night / low-light ambient, no dedicated fill. Key light frame right, three-quarter front, at subject height; lighting ratio about 1.8:1 (0.8 stops); medium-soft; colour temperature neutral (about 5000K); background sharp
- Grade: dark and desaturated (cinematic); low saturation, normal contrast, normal blacks, overall cool
- Editing: 37.8 cuts per minute, 1.44s average live-action shot; 4 camera set-up(s) / location(s); 0 jump cut(s) and 0 punch-in(s) within the same set-up; inserts (screenshots / screen recordings / cards / stock) take 76%
- Format: vlog (face as subject 80%, objects / hands as subject 20%, handheld 100%)
