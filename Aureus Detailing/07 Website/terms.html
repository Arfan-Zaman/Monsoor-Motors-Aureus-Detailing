Terms of service

Please read these before you book. By confirming an appointment with Aureus Detailing by Monsoor Motors, you accept these terms.

1. Our services

These terms apply whenever you book or use our detailing services, whether online, by phone or in person. We work in two ways:

  • Workshop detailing: you drop your vehicle at our Victoria Park workshop during your confirmed time window.
  • Mobile detailing: our equipped van comes to your home, workplace or another agreed location in the Perth area.

2. Bookings and time windows

Online bookings are made for a set time window. We aim to start at the beginning of your window, but detailing times vary with the vehicle's condition, size and package.

To reschedule or cancel, please give us at least 12 hours' notice so we can offer the time to another customer.

The same price applies within 20 km of our workshop. For addresses further away, we will phone you before the appointment to agree a travel fee.

3. Access and safety for mobile detailing

For a mobile appointment, you are responsible for providing:

  • a safe, legal and level place where our van can park next to your vehicle;
  • access to the vehicle for the whole service;
  • an outdoor water tap and a standard 240 V power outlet within reasonable distance, unless a self-powered setup has been arranged in advance.

4. Vehicle condition, valuables and existing damage

Please remove personal belongings, cash, electronics and valuables before we arrive or before drop-off. Our staff take care, but we cannot accept liability for personal property left in the vehicle.

We inspect the vehicle with you before we start and note existing paint defects, heavy swirl marks, stone chips, clearcoat failure, cracked leather and oxidised trim. We are not liable for existing damage, for repainted surfaces with poor adhesion, or for aftermarket parts that fail because they were already degraded.

5. Prices and payment

Prices on this website are in Australian dollars and include 10% GST. A surcharge may apply for very heavily soiled vehicles, for example severe pet hair, mould, biohazards or construction mud. We will agree any surcharge with you before work starts.

Payment is due when the work is complete, by cash, EFTPOS or credit card, or bank transfer. We provide a tax invoice and receipt.

6. If you are not satisfied

If any area does not meet the standard you expect, tell our team when the job is finished or contact us within 24 hours. We will inspect it and re-clean the area free of charge.

7. Australian Consumer Law

Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Nothing in these terms limits or changes your rights under Australian law.
