## Clip prompts (all passed `seedance_lint.py`)
### A
Setting: a dark deep-navy macro studio inside a luxury website interface, lit by cool aqua light. One continuous cinematic shot of a premium car detailing website, no cuts. Start on the homepage: the deep-navy SUV in darkness with a round glass lens over its bonnet. The camera pushes slowly into the lens until it fills the frame, and we fall through it into the paint at microscopic scale: metallic flakes glitter like a field of stars, silver swirl-mark canyons glide past below. A glowing aqua polishing arc sweeps across the landscape and melts the canyons flat into a mirror-smooth surface that reflects the aqua light. The frosted-glass header stays fixed at the top; the headline and the frosted-glass price panel settle gently into place. Smooth, slow, calm and expensive motion. Soft low synth hum and a glassy whoosh. No foam, no water spray, no people, no new text.

### B
Setting: a dark deep-navy macro studio inside a luxury website interface, lit by cool aqua light. One continuous cinematic shot of a premium car detailing website, no cuts. Start on the mirror-smooth paint surface with the price panel. The camera tilts down and sinks through the polished surface; the paint opens up into a dramatic cross-section of glowing layers: a deep-navy base coat full of glittering metallic flakes, a thick glass-clear clear coat, and a thin crystalline ceramic top layer catching aqua light like ice. Three frosted-glass package cards rise into place, each linked to its layer by a thin aqua line; the centre Gold card lifts slightly and brightens. The frosted-glass header stays fixed at the top. Smooth, slow, calm and expensive motion. Soft low synth hum and a crystalline shimmer. No foam, no water spray, no people, no new text.

### C
Setting: a dark deep-navy macro studio inside a luxury website interface, lit by cool aqua light. One continuous cinematic shot of a premium car detailing website, no cuts. Start on the cross-section of paint layers with the three package cards. A lattice of transparent ceramic crystal grows outward from the Gold card across the top surface, glinting aqua; the camera glides up over the crystal-coated paint, and a single perfect water bead drops in, lands and beads up into a round hydrophobic sphere reflecting the scene. A frosted-glass calendar and a confirmation card slide in from the sides, the morning time slot glows aqua, and a crystal tick grows on the confirmation card. The frosted-glass header stays fixed at the top. Smooth, slow, calm and expensive motion. Soft low synth hum, a gentle water drop and a bright crystal chime at the end. No foam, no people, no new text.
