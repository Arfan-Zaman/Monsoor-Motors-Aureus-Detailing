# Aureus scroll film: prompts (official Higgsfield scroll-scrub method)

Written 17 Nov 2026. One continuous ~15 s film plays as the visitor scrolls; the website's real text (headline, prices, packages, Book) sits over it as HTML. There is **no text in the film**.

**Settings:** storyboard = Nano Banana Pro, 16:9, 2K (2 credits), with the 4 approved keyframes as style references. Film = Seedance 2.5, mode omni_reference, the storyboard as a style reference (not a start frame), 15 s, 1080p, 16:9, audio off (240 credits). Higgsfield project: Aureus Detailing.

## Storyboard prompt (6 panels)
```
A single 16:9 storyboard image laid out as a clean 3-by-2 grid of six panels showing six keyframes of ONE continuous, unbroken cinematic camera move (NOT six different scenes), read left to right, top to bottom. No text, no numbers, no logos, no captions anywhere. Look locked in every panel: deep navy (#071A33) darkness, cool aqua (#00A8A8) light, deep-navy metallic paint with glittering metallic flake, macro-photography realism, calm and expensive, locked exposure. Panel 1: a sleek deep-navy metallic luxury SUV with no badges emerging from pure darkness, three-quarter front view, a thin aqua rim light tracing its bonnet, centred. Panel 2: the camera has pushed in close over the bonnet, the paint filling the frame. Panel 3: macro inside the paint at microscope scale, metallic flakes glittering like a field of stars, fine silver swirl-mark scratches across the surface. Panel 4: a glowing aqua polishing arc sweeping across the macro surface, scratched on one side, flawless mirror on the other. Panel 5: the camera sinking below the mirror surface, the paint revealed as glowing layers: navy base coat with flake, glass-clear clear coat, thin crystalline ceramic top. Panel 6: a transparent ceramic crystal lattice covering the surface, a single perfect round water bead resting on top reflecting aqua light. Centre-safe composition in every panel with dark negative space for text.
```

## Film prompt (one continuous 15 s shot)
```
One continuous, unbroken 15-second cinematic shot, no cuts, no camera shake, slow steady motion at constant speed, gentle ease only at the very start and end, locked exposure and white balance, no flicker, minimal motion blur, no on-screen text, no logos, no watermarks. Grade: deep navy #071A33 shadows, cool aqua #00A8A8 highlights, dark seamless background throughout. Begin: a sleek deep-navy metallic luxury SUV with no badges slowly emerges from pure darkness, three-quarter front view, centred, a thin aqua rim light tracing the bonnet. The camera glides forward and pushes smoothly into the bonnet until the paint fills the frame and becomes a macro world: glittering metallic flakes like a field of stars under fine silver swirl-mark scratches. A glowing aqua polishing arc sweeps steadily across the surface, leaving a flawless mirror finish behind it. The camera keeps sinking through the mirror surface into a cross-section of glowing paint layers: deep-navy base coat full of flake, a glass-clear clear coat, a thin crystalline ceramic top layer. End: a transparent ceramic crystal lattice grows across the surface and a single perfect round water bead settles on top, reflecting aqua light, the camera coming to rest. Keep the subject centred with dark negative space around it for text.
```
