    Before you book.

    The short answers. The full detail is in our terms.

    What do you need from me for a mobile detail?

    A safe, level spot where our van can park next to your car, access to the car for the whole job, and an outdoor tap and a standard power point nearby. If you don't have water or power, tell us in the notes and we'll arrange a self-powered setup.

    How and when do I pay?

    When the job is finished, by card, EFTPOS or cash. There's no deposit. Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST, and you get a tax invoice.

    Can I change or cancel my booking?

    Yes. Give us at least 12 hours' notice and we'll move or cancel it at no charge, so the slot can go to someone else.

    Could the price change on the day?

    Only if the car is far dirtier than usual, for example heavy pet hair, mould or construction mud. We'll tell you the extra cost before we start, and nothing happens until you agree.

    What if I'm not happy with the result?

    Tell us at handover or within 24 hours. We'll inspect the area and redo it free. This sits alongside your rights under Australian Consumer Law.

    Should I take anything out of the car?

    Please remove valuables, cash and electronics. We check the car over with you before we start and note any existing scratches, chips or worn trim.
