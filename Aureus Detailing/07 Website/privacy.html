Privacy policy

We collect only what we need to book and carry out your detail, and we never sell your details.

1. Our commitment

Aureus Detailing by Monsoor Motors respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and disclose personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

2. What we collect

When you book a detail or ask for a quote, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Vehicle details: vehicle size, make, model, year and registration.
  • Service location: the address for a mobile detail, or your workshop drop-off time.
  • Payment records: payment method, amount and invoice records. We do not store card numbers.
  • Messages: quote requests, feedback and other correspondence with our team.

3. How we use it

  • To schedule, confirm and carry out your appointment.
  • To send our mobile van to your address.
  • To send booking confirmations, updates and tax invoices by email or SMS.
  • To reply to enquiries and quote requests.
  • To improve our service and keep the website secure.

4. How we protect it

We limit access to your information to the staff who need it and take reasonable steps to protect it from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. We never sell, rent or trade your personal information to marketing companies.

5. Service providers

We use trusted providers for email delivery and website hosting, and this page embeds a Google map of our workshop. These providers process only what they need to deliver their service.

6. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The embedded Google map may set its own cookies when it loads. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your contact details from our records. Contact us at any time.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or complaints, contact us directly.
