## Prompt

Professional UX motion storyboard sheet for a luxury car detailing website called "Aureus Detailing", presented as a clean 4-column by 3-row grid of 12 numbered frames on a deep navy (#04101F) board, each frame a 16:9 photorealistic website screen with a one-line caption in small white sans-serif text underneath and thin aqua (#00A8A8) motion arrows drawn over the screens to show movement. Consistent look in every frame: a dark luxury wash bay at night, thin aqua LED light bars, a mirror-wet floor, frosted wet-glass interface panels with real water droplets, a 3D polished chrome letter "A" logo with a small hot-pink cap in the header, an elegant Roman-inscription serif for headlines, and hot-pink (#FF4D8D) buttons. Car: a sleek deep-navy metallic luxury electric SUV, no badges, no number plate text.

Frames:
1. "Lights on": black screen, the aqua LED bars flicker on one by one across the ceiling.
2. "Foam reveal": the SUV rises out of the dark covered in thick white snow foam; headline "Detailing, perfected." condenses onto the glass like breath.
3. "Rinse it yourself": a cursor shaped like a pressure-wash nozzle sprays a water jet across the bonnet, foam blasting away, ripples spreading across the glass.
4. "Clean and glossy": the car is fully rinsed, mirror gloss, water beads; a frosted-glass price panel slides in with "Sedan / SUV / Van" and "Silver / Gold / Aureus".
5. "Price rolls": the price digits roll like a car odometer from "$149" to "$199"; a hot-pink "Book this" button glows.
6. "Our work": scrolling down, a squeegee blade wipes the section heading clean; a video player shows a polisher on a door with four tabs below.
7. "Packages": three tall glass bays side by side, each lit by an aqua LED bar from above, labelled Silver $149, Gold $199, Aureus $299.
8. "Pick Gold": a squeegee stroke wipes the Gold card clean, revealing its list of inclusions; small water droplets splash off as add-ons are ticked.
9. "Pick a time": a frosted-glass calendar; the chosen day ripples; available time slots fill up like water rising in a glass.
10. "Your details": form fields on wet glass; the active field glows with an aqua ripple outline.
11. "Booked": a circle fills with water and turns into a white tick; a short "Booking request received" message on frosted glass.
12. "Find us": a map of Victoria Park, Perth, with a water-drop pin landing at 530 Albany Hwy and sending out ripples; footer with the chrome A logo.

Style: polished design-agency presentation board, cinematic lighting, crisp readable text, calm premium mood, colour palette limited to navy, aqua, ice white and small touches of hot pink.

Avoid: car brand logos or badges, real number plates, gold or yellow colours, people, cluttered or misspelled text, watermarks.
