# Website references for Aureus Detailing

## Octaboot: 3D scroll car wash website
- **Files:** `Octaboot - 3D scroll car wash website (DcYSCVeOgbP).mp4` (25 s, 720x1280), a frame preview, and the post details (`.info.json`).
- **Source:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcYSCVeOgbP/ by Octaboot (@octaboot.lb). Their caption: "Once users see the effort you put into your business, they become clients."
- **Downloaded:** 15 Nov 2026. This is for reference and inspiration only; it isn't ours to reuse as-is.

### What's worth borrowing
- A dark, premium page where a luxury car moves through a **tunnel of white light bars** as you scroll (scroll-driven 3D or video).
- Headlights sweeping on, bright reflections on black paint, and the car turning to show front, side and rear.
- Big, bold headlines layered over the car (e.g. "Back under the lights"), with short punchy lines.
- A quick **stats strip**: service time ("17 min"), rating ("4.9"), and a "Zero" claim.
- **Service cards** below the hero ("Full Hand Detail", "Paint Correction" style) with a clear call to action.

### How it maps to Aureus
- Navy instead of black, with the chrome 3D "A" logo and deep-aqua light bars echoing the logo's reflection.
- Hot pink only on "Book now".
- Use real, checkable numbers in the stats (actual service times, real review rating once you have one). Made-up figures would be misleading advertising.

## Marcelo Cedeno: modern website showcase
- **Files:** `Marcelo Cedeno - modern website showcase incl. Ferrari car page (DURcP3qj4mq).mp4` (13 s, 1080x1920), a frame preview, and the post details (`.info.json`).
- **Source:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DURcP3qj4mq/ by Marcelo Cedeno (@marcelodesignxh). Caption: "This is how your Website should look like."
- **Downloaded:** 15 Nov 2026. This is for reference and inspiration only.

### What's worth borrowing
- **Ferrari page (the most relevant):** a dark, dashboard-style car page with glowing headlights, side panels of small stats or controls, and a slide indicator at the bottom. Good for showing before-and-after or package details over a car.
- **Huge headline type behind a 3D object** (the "Probiotic" and "Comfort" pages): the product sits in front of giant letters. For Aureus, the car or the 3D "A" could sit in front of "DETAILING".
- **Soft gradient backgrounds and clean, roomy layouts.**
