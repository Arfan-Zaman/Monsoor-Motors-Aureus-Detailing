# Aureus Detailing: brand basics

Sister brand of Monsoor Motors (mobile and on-site car wash, detailing and polish).
Workshop: 530 Albany Hwy, Victoria Park WA 6100 · 0434 13 73 93

## Name
- **Aureus Detailing** (or Aureus Auto Spa), with "by Monsoor Motors" small underneath.
- Aureus is Latin for "golden", the gold coin of ancient Rome: gold-standard care for your car.

| Light | Ice white | #F5F8FC | as needed | Headlines and body text on navy, light backgrounds |

### Readability rules (contrast checked)
- On navy, every colour reads well: white 16.4:1, deep aqua 5.9:1, pink 5.6:1.
- On pink buttons, use **navy text** (5.6:1). White text on pink is too weak (2.9:1).
- On aqua, use **navy text** (5.9:1).
- On white backgrounds, don't use aqua (2.8:1) or pink (2.9:1) for small text; use them for shapes, accents and large type only.
