Before you start
  • Him: navy Aureus polo with aqua tipping, charcoal trousers, black work shoes. Black gloves for wet work. No cap, no sunglasses.
  • Car: not maroon. Pearl white, gloss black or a strong colour. Customer's written OK.
  • Kit in shot: Aureus van (logo side), trolley with the chrome bell, foam cannon, pressure washer, polisher with orange pad, wax pad, wheel brushes, steamer, aqua towels.
  • When: golden hour. An hour after sunrise or 1–1.5 h before sunset.
  • Where: a leafy client driveway, plus one Perth skyline shot (Kings Park lookout or South Perth foreshore). Water-capture mat near any stormwater drain.
Phone settings
  • Vertical, 4K, 30 fps. HDR video off. No filters.
  • Tap and hold to lock focus and exposure. Into the sun, expose for the bright spray.
  • 0.5× for low hero angles, 1× for most, 2–3× for talking and details.
  • Roll 1 s before the action, hold 1 s after. At least 5 s, 2–3 takes.
  • Keep sound on every clip. For lines: clip-on mic or phone 30 cm away, no wind.
  • Name clips by code if you can, like A04-1. If not, send everything.
