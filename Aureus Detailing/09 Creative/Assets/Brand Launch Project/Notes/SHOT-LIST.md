# Brand launch reels: shoot list (real footage)

One shoot, three reels. Every shot copies a reference reel's setup (`../Brand Launch Reel References/`): its angle, lens, light and movement. The words, car and location are ours. No footage from earlier Aureus reels is reused. The edit is free on the Mac (`make_reels.py`).

## Before the shoot
- **Who:** the master detailer (our trainer) in the uniform from the character board: navy Aureus polo with aqua tipping, charcoal trousers, black work shoes, black nitrile gloves for wet work. Clean-shaven, no cap, no sunglasses.
- **Car:** a different colour from earlier reels (not maroon): **pearl white, gloss black or a strong colour**. A customer's car with their written OK. Plates are blurred in the edit, but still avoid lingering on them.
- **Kit in shot:** Aureus van (logo side visible), detailing trolley with the **chrome service bell**, snow foam cannon, pressure washer, machine polisher with orange pad, wax applicator, wheel brushes, steamer, aqua microfibre towels.
- **Where (Perth):** a client's driveway in a leafy suburb (gum trees, brick paving), plus one **Perth skyline** shot (Kings Park lookout, or South Perth foreshore at Mends St). For the cinematic reel, an open, sunny space with a low sun behind the car works best: a quiet open-air car park top level **with the owner's OK**. Use a water-capture mat near any stormwater drain (Perth councils don't allow wash runoff into drains).
- **When:** golden hour. About an hour after sunrise or 1–1.5 h before sunset. Low sun, long shadows, sun behind the car for the backlit spray shots.

## Camera settings (phone is fine)
- **Vertical 9:16, 4K, 30 fps.** HDR video **off** (SDR), no filters, no beauty mode. Lock focus and exposure (tap and hold). On backlit shots, expose for the bright spray.
- Lenses: **0.5×** for low hero angles (Reel C), **1×** for most shots, **2–3×** for talking shots and details (softer background, like Ref 2).
- Gimbal or steady hands, slow moves only. **Start recording 1 s before the action and hold 1 s after.** At least 5 s per clip, 2–3 takes.
- **Sound on every clip:** foam, spray, polisher hum, brush and bell sounds are used in the edit. For spoken lines, a clip-on mic (or the phone 30 cm from his mouth), no wind, no traffic.

## How to name the files
Name each clip with its code, e.g. `A04-1.mov` (shot A04, take 1). If that's too fiddly on the day, just send everything and the clips will be matched to the list.
