# The master detailer: persona (set 17 Nov 2026)

Original character. Inspired by the screen archetype of the stern, silent old boss (owner's reference: Hector Salamanca, Breaking Bad), but **not a copy**: no name, no wheelchair, no crime references, no lines or scenes from the show.

- **Few words.** Short, gruff, dry. Never a sales pitch. Says three words where others say twenty.
- **The glare.** Squints hard at paint like it owes him money. Unimpressed until the job is perfect.
- **The bell.** Carries a small chrome service bell on his detailing trolley. Taps it once, "ding", when a step passes his inspection. That ding is the brand's sound.
- **The grin.** Only once per video, at the very end, a small reluctant grin. It lands because it is rare.
- **Voice.** Low, gravelly, slow, relaxed Australian. Dry humour, never shouting.
- **Lines he would say:** "Nah." · "Again." · "Every spoke. By hand." · "Yeah. That'll do." · "Done properly, or it's not done."
