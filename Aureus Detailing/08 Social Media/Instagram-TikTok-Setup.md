## 6. Rules (Australian law and platform rules)
- **No fake reviews or testimonials.** Australian Consumer Law and the ACCC prohibit them.
- **No invented numbers** (customer counts, insurance amounts) until confirmed. The $10M insurance claim is still unconfirmed.
- **AI images must be labelled.** Instagram and TikTok both require it, and the hero car is an AI Porsche Macan with no Porsche affiliation.
- **Get permission** before showing customers' cars or faces; blur number plates.
- **Staff in videos:** get each person's OK before posting.
- **Prices** shown must match the booking page (drive-away style: no hidden extras).

## 7. Account creation steps (the owner does these, on the business phone)

**Instagram**
1. Install Instagram → *Create new account* → choose to sign up with email (`info@monsoormotors.com.au`).
2. Enter the verification code from the inbox and create the password (password manager).
3. Username: `aureusdetailing.com.au`. Upload the profile picture.
4. Settings → *Account type and tools* → *Switch to professional account* → **Business** → category *Car Wash*.
5. Fill in the name, bio, contact buttons and link from section 2.
6. Settings → *Accounts Centre* → *Password and security* → turn on **two-factor authentication**.
7. Optional: link it to the Monsoor Motors Facebook Page (Accounts Centre) for ads and Meta Business Suite.

**TikTok**
1. Install TikTok → *Sign up* → *Use phone or email* → email `info@monsoormotors.com.au`.
2. Enter the verification code and create the password.
3. Username: `aureusdetailing.com.au`. Name: `Aureus Detailing Perth`. Upload the profile picture.
4. Profile → ☰ → *Settings and privacy* → *Account* → **Switch to Business Account** → category *Auto & Vehicle*.
5. Add the bio and website link from section 3.
6. *Security* → turn on **2-step verification**.
