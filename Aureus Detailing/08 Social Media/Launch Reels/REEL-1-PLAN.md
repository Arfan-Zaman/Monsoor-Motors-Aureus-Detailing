# Launch reel 1 plan: "Gold on a maroon BMW M3"

Written 17 Nov 2026. Plan only; nothing is generated until the owner approves. Hard cap: **30 Higgsfield credits** for this reel.

## What the research says (car-detailing reels)
- The first 3 seconds decide retention; lead with the strongest visual and a number.
- The formats repeated across guides: transformation/reveal, close-up process shots with real sound (ASMR), and short educational "what you get" clips.
- Hooks with numbers and on-screen text beat POV-style openers in a study of about 150,000 cross-posted videos.
- Macro shot first, then the reveal, is a known retention device.
- The numbers in these sources are vendor claims; our own 3-second retention is the real test.

## Format
9:16, 1080×1920, about 22 seconds, captions on screen throughout (works with sound off), text kept clear of the top 250 px and bottom 400 px.

That leaves 8 credits for one retake (a 4–6 s speaking retake costs 6–9, a still costs 2).

## His line (8 seconds, about 22 words)
"This is our Gold detail. Machine polish, wax sealant, interior steam clean and leather care. Two and a half hours. One ninety-nine."
Voice direction: Australian man in his sixties, warm, unhurried, tradesman-honest.

## Why one speaking clip only
Voice and lips made in one generation give the cleanest sync. Two separate clips would risk two slightly different voices, so all his speech sits in a single 8-second take.

## Free work done on this Mac (no credits)
Camera moves on stills, cuts, captions in Montserrat and Cormorant, the end card, and export, all with ffmpeg and Python. Music: add a licensed track inside Instagram/Facebook when posting.

## Checks before it's called finished
- Frame-by-frame check of the speaking clip: lips match every word, face matches the board, hands and uniform logo correct. If not clean, one retake; if still not clean, stop and report instead of spending more.
- Every claim matches the website: Gold inclusions, $199 sedan price, 2 h 30, the launch offer wording.

## Rules that apply
- Label the reel as AI-generated on Instagram and Facebook (required; the car, the scenes and the voice are AI).
- The BMW is an illustration, not a customer job: no "before/after result" claims, and the caption says so.
- The voice is synthetic, not his real voice. He has agreed to his face being used; confirm he's also fine with an AI voice.
