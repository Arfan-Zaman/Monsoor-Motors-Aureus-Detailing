# Aureus cinematic ad: "The shine comes to you" (plan, 17 Nov 2026)

Builds on launch reel 1. Same detailer, van, maroon M3 and driveway, so nothing already paid for is wasted. Reel 1 was stills with captions; this version is moving film, a narrator, a story, animated explanations and a cover image.

**Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 seconds, 24 fps. Text and faces stay out of the top 250 px and bottom 400 px.

## The story (problem → arrival → craft → reveal → offer)
A car owner's paint looks tired in the Perth sun, and he has no Saturday to spare. Aureus arrives at his driveway. We watch the craft up close, with on-screen graphics explaining what each step does. The car is revealed like new. Offer and booking.

## Sound

- **Effects:** polisher hum, steam hiss, water, soft whooshes on cuts.
