# Aureus ad v2: director's brief and direction (17 Nov 2026)

## The brief
- **Client:** Aureus Detailing (Monsoor Motors Pty Ltd), mobile car detailing, Perth.
- **Job:** a 30-second vertical (9:16) cinema-grade ad for Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, built from ad v1.
- **Offer:** Gold detail $199 · 2 h 30 · at your place · launch offer $25 off for the first 100 customers · within 20 km of Victoria Park.
- **One message:** a perfectionist comes to your driveway and won't leave until your car is right.
- **Must feel:** a big-budget car commercial that is completely natural. Nobody should think "AI" for a second.

## The character
The master detailer, the only person on screen, in every service shot. Persona in `../../Character Board/PERSONA.md`: few words, a perfectionist glare, a chrome service bell he taps "ding" when a step passes, one rare grin at the end. Low, gravelly, relaxed Australian voice, dry, never salesy.

## Direction
- **Story:** problem (swirls in the Perth sun, his glare: "Nah.") → he arrives → he says the offer to camera → three services, each shown from **two angles with two different techniques** → the bell → the paint close-up, now flawless ("Yeah. That'll do.") → rare grin → offer.
- **Camera:** shot as if on an ARRI Alexa 35 with anamorphic and 50 mm lenses. Handheld with slight natural sway and slow push-ins; low hero angles on the car; macro for the paint. No impossible camera moves.
- **Motion:** every shot plays at **real speed** (never sped up or slowed down). Real hand movement, real water, real steam.
- **Light and grade:** golden-hour sun throughout. One shared grade on every shot: gentle filmic contrast, warm highlights, slightly cool shadows, natural skin, fine film grain, soft vignette. Brand colours appear only in graphics, never painted onto the footage.
- **Sound:** his voice up front; the bell "ding" as the signature sound on each passed step; an original 100 bpm score under him; real effects (polisher, steam, water); cuts on the beat.
- **Graphics:** see-through glass panels in brand navy, aqua and pink; text kept inside the safe area (clear of the top 250 px and bottom 400 px); "Step 1–3 of 3" labels; animated timer and price count-up.
- **Watermark:** the Aureus logo top left, about 210 px wide, 50% opacity, on every shot except the end card.
